# Brand

## Voice
Encouraging, never preachy. A running partner, not a coach yelling. Short sentences. Numbers before words.

- Yes: "5.2 km today, 800 m more than last week."
- No: "Amazing!! You're the best runner ever!!"

## Copy rules
- English. Units: km, min. Pace is written `5'42"`.
- Buttons are verbs: Start, Pause, End, Save.
- No exclamation marks in system copy. One is allowed in a personal-best message.
