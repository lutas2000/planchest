# Interview script: brand

Writes `BRAND.md` and copies logo files into `system/brand/`. Ask in this order. Each round is one message.

## Round 1. Name and naming

1. The exact spelling and casing of the product name, and of any feature names that already exist. Is there a short form?
2. How are new features named: plain nouns ("Goals"), branded nouns ("Stride Plans"), verbs ("Plan a run")? (Default: plain nouns.)
3. Is there a tagline or a one-line description that appears in the product?

→ Name and naming.

## Round 2. Voice

4. Three adjectives for how the product sounds. Offer sets and let the user pick or edit: "calm, precise, warm" · "direct, upbeat, plain" · "quiet, expert, dry" · "friendly, chatty, encouraging".
5. Does the product say "we"? Does it say "you"? (Default: "you" for the user, no "we".)
6. Formality and humour: would it ever make a joke? Use an exclamation mark? Use emoji? (Default: no jokes, exclamation marks only for success, no emoji.)
7. How does the voice shift in: onboarding, an error, an empty state, a success, a legal notice? One phrase each, or "same voice, shorter".

→ Voice and tone.

## Round 3. Copy rules

8. Buttons: verbs ("Save run") or nouns ("Run")? Sentence case or Title Case? (Default: verbs, sentence case.)
9. Errors: should they say what happened, why, and what to do next? Which of the three can be left out? (Default: what happened and what to do next.)
10. Numbers, dates, units: "5 km" or "5km", "Thu 8 Oct" or "08/10", 24-hour or 12-hour, decimal comma or point?
11. Sentence length and punctuation habits: serial comma, ampersands, ellipses, trailing periods on labels?

→ Copy rules.

## Round 4. Logo

12. Do you have logo files? Paths, please (SVG preferred; a dark-background version if there is one). If none, skip the rest of this round and the Logo section keeps its placeholder.
13. Which file goes on which background? Minimum size? Clear space around it?
14. What must never be done with it (recolour, rotate, put on a photo, add a tagline)?

→ Logo; files copied into `system/brand/`.

## Round 5. Examples

15. From the answers so far, draft three "Write / Not" pairs for the Examples table (an error, a button, an empty state) and ask the user to correct them. Ask for one more pair from their own experience if they have one.

→ Examples.
